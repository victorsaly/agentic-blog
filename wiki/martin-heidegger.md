# Martin Heidegger

Heidegger was a philosopher. His essay *The Question Concerning Technology* looks at how technology changes the relationship between people and the world. The sources I have do not give the essay's date, so check it before this page goes live.

## Two words from the essay

- **Enframing** (*das Gestell*): a way of seeing in which everything is judged by what it can do for us. The Stanford entry says the word is hard to translate.
- **Standing reserve**: the result of that way of seeing, where everything becomes a resource waiting to be used. The Wikipedia overview says this includes humans.

## Why it matters to this post

The post contrasts working with nature with an industrial power station, where a river is only a source of energy. It then asks what happens when we look at people the same way. A customer becomes a conversion, an employee becomes a capacity, and a user's attention becomes engagement. We already see this language in dashboards and reports.

In the post's argument, an agent optimises whatever it is told to optimise. If people are measured as resources, the agent treats them as resources. The post says this is a problem in the design of the system, not in the AI model.
