# Aristotle

Aristotle was a philosopher. The post places him "two thousand years ago". The sources I have do not give his dates, so check them before this page goes live.

## What he said that matters here

He described four causes behind everything that exists:

- what it is made of (material)
- what form it takes (formal)
- who made it (efficient)
- what it is for (final)

He sets this out in the *Physics* and the *Metaphysics*. The Stanford entry has a section on the final cause, which means purpose. It explains why Aristotle ranks it so high.

## Why it matters to this post

The post uses a cup as its example. The cup is ceramic, it has a shape, and someone made it. Its purpose is to drink. In the post's words, someone has to imagine drinking before they design the cup, so the purpose comes first and the object is that purpose made solid.

From this the post argues that technology is not neutral, because even a cup is a decision about what matters. An agent is the same: it starts with someone deciding what they want it to do.
