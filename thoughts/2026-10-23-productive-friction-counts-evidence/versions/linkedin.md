We hide AI-assisted work like it is cheating. I see it as an enhanced way of producing results.

So what counts as evidence of competence? What do we reward? The job goal must change.

I am very pro AI. It brings incredible things to anyone who has thoughts and can turn them into real working ideas. But the world is changing so quickly that we are just catching up, and it is tiring.

The skills bar is raising. Expectations are results that go directly to the point, not a generic one. We humans turn long windy results into small, clear answers. And the one who decides whether something sits or not is still the human.

My advice: think first, act after. Spend hours, days, on what you want, so the tool gets the right direction. Learn how AI works and how it will make mistakes. Keep the core of your identity.

AI will not exist without us humans.
