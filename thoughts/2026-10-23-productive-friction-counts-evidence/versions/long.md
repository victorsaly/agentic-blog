# What counts as evidence of competence

## We hide the work, and we should stop

We tend to hide AI-assisted work. We treat it like cheating.

I see it differently. It is an enhanced way of producing results.

I want my product to look more human, more unique. Not slop.

## What we reward

So what counts as evidence of competence? What do we reward?

If the work is assisted, then the old answer does not hold. The job goal must change.

## Fundamentals still matter

The skills have to change. But the right fundamentals matter for every piece of work, however much it is assisted.

Without a good understanding, your final judgement becomes hands of the wave. It is no longer your final architecture.

## The bar is raising

The skills bar is raising. That is the world we live in now.

Expectations are not only about a result. The result that matters goes directly to the point. It is not redirected to a generic one.

The long windy results come from models built on statistics and closed assumptions. This is where we humans, the flesh-based ones, come in. We turn those long answers into small, clear ones.

## The human decides

The one who makes the decision is still the human, at least in the current world.

As a programmer, my goal is a framework that gives us accountability for the important steps. At those steps, we decide whether something sits or not. We guide the solution from our area of expertise.

## Why something human will stand out

Most social media today is AI-driven. In a way it becomes monotonous, boring, not unique.

When someone comes with something new, it is exciting and appealing.

At some point, I think we will find thoughts and outcomes that are not AI-driven very exotic, expensive and elegant.

## I am pro AI, and the pace has a cost

I am very pro AI. It brings incredible things to anyone who has thoughts and can turn them into real working ideas. That is amazing, and it is a good time to be alive.

But this pace will come with consequences if we do not look at it. There are already signs. The world is changing so quickly that we are just catching up, and it is tiring.

The old way was to let the process drive the learning. At this pace, that is hard to do.

## My advice

Don't let AI break your unique way of thinking. Embrace your idea with it.

Keep the core of your identity. Say you are a doctor interested in holistic medicine. That mixture of different things is your core. It is what makes you unique.

Think first, act after. Building ideas is easy now, so the thinking matters more. Spend hours, even days, on what you want. Then you can give the tool the right direction.

Learn how AI works. How it thinks, how it will react, how it will make mistakes. How valid a mistake can be, so you deliver first rather than wait for perfection. Understand the limits.

AI will not exist without us humans.
