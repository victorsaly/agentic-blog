We hide AI-assisted work as if it were cheating. I see it as a better way of producing results.

So what counts as evidence of competence? What do we reward? The job goal must change.

I am very pro AI. It brings incredible things to anyone who has thoughts and can turn them into working ideas. But the world is changing so quickly that we are just catching up, and it is tiring.

The skills bar is raising. Without good fundamentals, your final judgement becomes hands of the wave, and not your final architecture. Results must go straight to the point, not to a generic one. We humans turn long windy answers into small, clear ones.

As a programmer, I want a framework with accountability at the important steps. The human decides whether something sits or not.

I want my product to look more human, more unique, not slop.

My advice: don't let AI break your unique way of thinking. Embrace your idea with it. Keep the core of your identity.

Think first, act after. Spend hours, even days, thinking about what you want, so you give the tool the right direction. Learn how AI works, how it thinks, how it will make mistakes.

AI will not exist without us humans.
