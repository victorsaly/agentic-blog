A year ago, AI could not build my son's game. Today I am almost sure it would.

In September last year we tried to build WorldLeadersGame, his idea: a retro, dice-based educational strategy game. I used only Copilot and wanted the AI to drive the development.

It started well. Then it kept shifting, or forgetting, the original request. After three to four weeks we stopped. It was a shame.

Two things have changed. The AI is better, and Claude in particular has shown a better approach. And I know much better how much to hand over, and how to organise my questions and reviews.

The proof is this blog. It is two days old. It already has more than 12 agents, a database and a web interface to talk to. 115 pull requests so far, and I review each one with before and after images.

I will prove the change by running the original project again.
