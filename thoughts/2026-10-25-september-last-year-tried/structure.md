## The idea

In September last year I tried to build a game with my son. The idea came from him, so we recorded it. It was a retro, dice-based educational strategy game. You had different types of people, and from their capabilities they earned money and won things. We called it WorldLeadersGame.

It started really well. I used only Copilot, and I wanted the AI to drive the development. But it was not able to answer issues properly. It kept shifting, or forgetting, the original request and orders. After three to four weeks we decided it was not capable of what we wanted. It was a shame.

Today I am almost certain we would get a better result, for two reasons. The coding standard has improved, Claude in particular has shown a better approach. And I understand much better how much leverage to give the AI, and how to organise my questions and reviews to get what I want.

This blog is the proof of the feeling. We started two days ago. It already has more than 12 agents, a database, and a web interface to talk to. 115 pull requests have been created, and I review each one with before and after images. Everything you see in this studio and blog is generated by agents and automations.

I will prove the change by running the original project again.
