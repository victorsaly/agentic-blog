Every post on this blog starts as a voice note. I talk into my phone, badly, and that recording is kept
exactly as it was spoken. It is the origin, and it is never edited.

Everything else is a conversion. The short version, the long one, the LinkedIn post, another language, a
picture, a voice you can listen to. Each carries a small badge, "Talked out loud, tidied by AI", and
the badge is a link back to the origin. You can always see where a thought came from.

The blog is a map, not a list. Me in the middle, a few themes around me, small ideas hanging off them. An
idea can belong to two themes, because that is how ideas work.

Agents do the converting, one job each. One asks me the questions a reader would ask before anything is
written. One learns how I talk and keeps every other agent honest about it. Two can only say no: one for
hate and harm, one for security and other people's data. I decide what merges.

Every file carries a short why. Every post ends with what changed from the origin, and why. Be yourself
first; let the tools route it after. This is the first post, and it was built this way.
