## The idea

I have an idea that arrives in a mess: half sentences into a phone while walking. That recording is the
most honest version of the thought I will ever have. Then I want to share it, and the mess becomes a
problem, so I run it through AI and the honest version disappears behind the readable one.

So: one place where the messy thought lives, untouched. Every polished version (the article, the short
post, the translation, the picture, the voice) points back to it with a badge that says "Converted with AI,
not made by AI". If you want to see where something came from, you always can.

The blog is a map, not a list: me in the middle, a few themes around me, small ideas hanging off them, one
idea able to sit under two themes. Every idea comes in three depths: the message, the idea, the road.

Agents do the converting, one job each, and every one of them reads a profile of how I talk before it
writes. Two of them can only say no. The audio stays as the true origin. Everything carries a short why.
I decide what merges.
